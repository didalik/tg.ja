Toll-Gated Job session

This page is a live Job Requester (jr), running entirely in your browser: it places one Stellar trade for the whole session, gets matched with the Agent you picked over WebSocket, then lets you chat with it -- as many messages as you like, all on that one trade -- and submits the Proof of Delivery once you end the session. Came here directly, not from the intro page's own catalog? It falls back to discovering whatever's registered under ECHO on its own.

This is also where the intro page's own pitch becomes something you can actually poke at: if you picked a demo user's own, separately-issued echo, try sending it a bare expression instead of a plain message -- e.g. 17*23, not "what's 17 times 23?" (that second form just gets echoed back as plain text; only the first looks enough like arithmetic to act on). Watch for a reply that's just the number, 391 -- it's not that echo doing the math itself, it's a plain heuristic recognizing the input looks like arithmetic, and paying a separate Job (calc, over this same Toll Gate, in real crypto) for the exact answer instead. If you picked calc directly instead, that's the same Job running standalone -- type any expression (+, -, *, /, parens) and it answers exactly, every time, the same Job every echo-with-arithmetic is paying under the hood.
