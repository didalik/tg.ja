The KoT Jobs: Matched on Cloudflare, Gated and Settled on Stellar

The Kloud of Trust (KoT) matches Job Requests with Job Agents on Cloudflare, and settles the deal between them on Stellar -- no platform in the middle taking a cut or holding the keys. What's running below isn't a mockup: two genuinely independent machines, each selling access to a real Job, trade and get paid entirely on-chain, without ever needing to see each other directly.

Known issue: Chrome on Android currently fails to open the WebSocket connection below -- the gate trade fills fine, then the session silently fails to match. Firefox (or any desktop browser) works fine -- use that if you're on Android, for now.

A Job Agent issues its own small Stellar asset and sells one unit of it at a fixed price, like a toll booth selling passage. The trade that buys it is the receipt: a public, timestamped Proof of Purchase (PoP), free because the Stellar DEX produces it as a side effect of the trade itself -- which also queues multiple Requesters one at a time. The Agent needs no public IP: a laptop behind a home router, even an Android phone running Termux, stays reachable 24/7, since it only ever makes outbound connections.

Paying the toll matches you with the Agent over a WebSocket, relayed through a single Cloudflare Durable Object's Hibernation API -- the whole Job runs without the Agent paying for idle compute between messages. Once it's done, the Requester pays the same asset straight back: an ordinary, unilateral Stellar payment that is Proof of Delivery (PoD). Two transactions, PoP and PoD, bookend every Job -- no escrow, no dispute, nobody in the middle.

The goal: wrap Cloudflare-based Job matching with Stellar-based cryptonomics, so anybody can offer a Job and get paid when it runs. This demo is the entry point -- request a Job (plain echo, below), join as a Job Agent, implement and offer your own Job, get paid once it's requested and run. If that's interesting to you, as a coder or a tester, I'd love to have you join us.
