Running the KoT Job: calc

Each KoT Job Request takes 6 steps to complete:

  1. Trust the Job Asset (A)
  2. Buy 1 unit of A for XLM
  3. Get the grant (on trade, PoP)
  4. Match the JR with a JA
  5. Relay the messages between JR and JA (start the Job)
  6. Produce PoD to stop the Job gracefully

Type any arithmetic expression (+, -, *, /, parens) and hit Send -- it answers exactly, every time. The reason for this Job to exist is that it is called by another Job, own-echo, which you will "implement" yourself once you join as a demo JA via the CSR Job from the home page.
