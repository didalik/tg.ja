Toll-Gated Job session: calc

This page is a live Job Requester (jr), running entirely in your browser: it places one real Stellar trade for the whole session, gets matched with JA2's real calc Job over WebSocket, then lets you chat with it -- as many messages as you like, all on that one trade -- and submits the Proof of Delivery once you end the session.

Type any arithmetic expression (+, -, *, /, parens) and hit Send -- it answers exactly, every time.
