the KoT Job: your own echo

Here, you "implement" your own ECHO Job: if the input is an arithmetic expression, it buys the CALC Job to output the result; otherwise, it echoes the input back. Two moments worth noting:

  • this makes your transient JA a Job Issuer (JI) -- your Job gets registered and is now visible within the KoT, and JIs can invite others to become JAs (JA2 presently being the only example);
  • your ECHO Job shows how KoT Jobs call each other -- note the execution delay.
