Toll-Gated Job session: your own echo

This page is a live Job Requester (jr), running entirely in your browser: it places one real Stellar trade for the whole session, gets matched with the Job Agent you just stood up by joining a few steps ago, then lets you chat with it -- as many messages as you like, all on that one trade -- and submits the Proof of Delivery once you end the session.

This one's always your own, self-issued identity, never the real Owner's and never a different reseller's -- so it always pays a separate, real Job (calc, in real crypto, opens in its own tab) for an exact answer whenever your message looks like plain arithmetic (e.g. 17*23, not "what's 17 times 23?") rather than guessing. Watch for a reply that's just the number, 391 -- that few-second wait is the delegation actually happening, not anything stuck.
