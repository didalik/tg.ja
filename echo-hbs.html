Running the KoT Job: echo

Each KoT Job Request takes 6 steps to complete:

  1. Trust the Job Asset (A)
  2. Buy 1 unit of A for XLM
  3. Get the grant (on trade, PoP)
  4. Match the JR with a JA
  5. Relay the messages between JR and JA (start the Job)
  6. Produce PoD to stop the Job gracefully
The only reason for this page to exist is to introduce the 6-step JR workflow.
