Running the KoT Job: echo

Each KoT Job Request takes 6 steps to complete:

  1. Trust the Job Asset (A)
  2. Buy 1 unit of A for XLM
  3. Get the grant (on trade, PoP)
  4. Match the JR with a JA
  5. Relay the messages between JR and JA (start the Job)
  6. Produce PoD to stop the Job gracefully
The only reason for this page to exist is to introduce the 6-step JR workflow with a real, however basic, KoT Job.

If step 2 is slow, or times out, that's most likely just popularity -- the Owner's own single standing offer serves one buyer at a time, so a lot of people trying this Job at once means some of you are waiting in line, not that anything's broken. Wait a minute or two and try again.
