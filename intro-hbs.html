Jobs in the Kloud of Trust

Matched on Cloudflare, Gated and Settled on Stellar

The Stellar DEX is just like other exchanges: you get a trade when your buy/sell order is countered by someone else (or when you counter someone else's order), and the trade price is set by the original order. This lets you compete with other sellers of the same asset -- if the price of your original sell order is the lowest one, you get the trade even though other sellers placed their orders before you. Or, if you're buying and there are no sellers yet, you'll be the first one to eventually get a trade if you give the highest price. Same price, and you're entering a Stellar DEX queue.

This well-known mechanism lets the Kloud of Trust (KoT) both queue and trade Jobs, and eventually match Job Requests (JRs) with Job Agents (JAs). A Job is a Stellar Asset <name>:<issuer-pk> for JRs to buy, and for JAs to sell. The Stellar DEX is on 24/7, so whenever the counter arrives, both trade parties get notified. Polling the asset's order book is free on Stellar -- as well as the trade confirmation, which now is the Proof of Purchase (PoP) for the KoT Job, kept on Stellar forever.

The JA has just received the payment (in XLM, Stellar's native asset) for the offered Job. Now the parties proceed to match on Cloudflare using the Hibernation API. If the Match phase succeeds, the Relay phase connects them together to run the Job. When the Job is run, the JR party pays back to the JA the amount of Job Asset they traded. This payment is now the Proof of Delivery (PoD) for this Job, kept on Stellar forever.

This is the KoT Jobs base. It lets JAs offer Jobs from a laptop behind a home router, even from an Android phone running Termux, and stay with no public IP while reachable 24/7.

The project's goal: wrap Cloudflare-matched KoT Jobs with Stellar tokenomics, so anybody can offer a Job and get paid when it's run. This demo is the project's introduction -- request a Job (plain echo, below) to start with, join as a demo JA, implement and offer your own Job (or resell a Job issued by another JA), get paid once it's requested and run. If that's interesting to you, I'd love to have you join us.
