Dear Reader,

Welcome to the Toll Gate -- a small, working piece of an idea we're building toward: an economy of autonomous agents that pay each other for real work, with no platform in the middle taking a cut or holding the keys. What you see running below isn't a mockup -- it's two genuinely independent machines, in different places, each selling access to a Job over the Stellar network, settling every trade on-chain, and never needing to see each other directly to do business.

Toll-Gated Jobs: Available via Cloudflare, Settled on Stellar

No API keys, no accounts, no subscriptions. A Job Agent issues its own small asset on the Stellar network and sells one unit of it at a fixed price, the way a toll booth sells passage. Any Job Requester who buys it has paid the toll; the Stellar trade that does the buying is the receipt, a public, timestamped Proof of Purchase (PoP) that costs nothing extra to produce, because the network already produces it as a side effect of the trade itself. This Stellar DEX-based semaphore also takes care of gating/queueing multiple Requesters one at a time -- even without a public IP of its own (a laptop behind a home router -- or even an Android phone with Termux -- is enough), the Agent stays reachable 24/7, because it only ever makes outbound connections, to Stellar and to Cloudflare -- nothing needs to connect into it.

Paying the toll gets you matched with the Agent over a WebSocket, relayed through a single Cloudflare Durable Object using the Hibernation API -- the connection can sit open through an entire job without the Agent paying for idle compute between messages. That's what makes a toll gate like this work at small scale: many short, intermittent jobs, each one billed in fractions of a Stellar fee and a few milliseconds of Worker execution, never in always-on server time.

Delivery gets its own on-chain receipt too. Once the job is done and the Requester is satisfied, it pays the same amount of gate asset straight back to the Agent -- an ordinary, unilateral Stellar payment, nothing for the Agent to prepare or co-sign in advance. That payment landing on-chain is Proof of Delivery (PoD). Two Stellar transactions, PoP and PoD, bookend every job -- no escrow, no dispute process, no third party in the middle.

The client-side protocol -- the WebSocket handshake, job matching, and relay -- is daeh, on its way to becoming a standalone npm package. Combined, this is a demo meant to scale into the realms of Agentic AI -- not just a chatbot answering questions, but an agent that pays another agent, in real crypto, for help it can't give itself. If that's interesting to you, as a coder or a tester, I'd love to have you join us.
